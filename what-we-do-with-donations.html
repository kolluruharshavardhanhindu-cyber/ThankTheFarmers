How Your Thanks Reaches Farmers

1
Step one

Initial Support for Farmers

We begin by selecting villages and collecting information from the respective Gram Panchayats about small and marginal farmers who own limited agricultural land, as well as farmers who cultivate leased land. Based on the collected information, we identify farmers who are genuinely in need of support.

Once identified, we extend our support through:

  • 🌱
    Providing high-quality seeds to improve crop growth and productivity.
  • 💰
    Offering financial assistance to purchase quality seeds.
  • 🌾
    Supplying fertilizers to support healthy crop cultivation.
  • 🚁
    Providing access to rented agricultural drones for spraying and crop monitoring.
  • 🚜
    Arranging other essential agricultural equipment to reduce the financial burden on farmers.
Our goal is to help small farmers access the resources they need to cultivate their land successfully without falling into additional debt.
2
Step two

Support for Farmers Facing Crop Loss

Agriculture is uncertain, and farmers can lose their entire investment due to unpredictable weather, pests, diseases, and other natural circumstances. When we identify farmers who have suffered significant crop losses, we step forward to help them recover and begin again.

Our support focuses on:

  • 🔍
    Identifying farmers who have experienced crop failure and financial losses.
  • 🤝
    Providing financial assistance to help them prepare for the next cultivation cycle.
  • 🌱
    Supplying quality seeds, fertilizers, and other essential farming resources.
  • 🔄
    Helping them restart cultivation without carrying the entire financial burden alone.
A failed crop should not mean a failed future.

We want every farmer to have another opportunity, renewed confidence, and the hope to grow again.
