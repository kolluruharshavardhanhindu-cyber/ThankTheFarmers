Small change, big thanks

Trade a Habit for a Thank You – Honour the Farmer Who Feeds You

We have no right to tell you to stop smoking, drinking alcohol, or giving up other habits. Those are your personal choices.

But what if you could reduce these habits, even just a little, and use the money you save to say thank you to the farmer who feeds you?

A small change in your spending could be a big thank you to a farmer.
1

Trade the Ash for the Harvest

Think about how much you spend on cigarettes in a week.

That same amount could thank a farmer by helping them buy fertilizers, seeds, or meet essential cultivation expenses.

Reduce a little. Give a little. Thank a farmer.
2

Trade the Bottle for the Basket

Think about the money you spend on alcohol every week.

That amount could become a thank-you gift that helps a small farmer buy essential equipment or begin the next cultivation cycle.

What you spend in a week could help the farmer who feeds you stand tall again.
